The Opportunity

Venus's clouds at 50-60km altitude offer Earth-like conditions: 1 atm pressure, 20-30°C temps. Potential for life and human habitation.

Project AETHER

A balloon probe with sensors for atmospheric analysis, life detection, and imaging. Launch in 2026 via rideshare.

Market Potential

Advancing space tech for Venus colonization. Partnerships with NASA, ESA. Revenue from data, tech licensing.

Our Team

Experienced engineers from SpaceX, NASA. Led by founder with track record in aerospace startups.

Traction

Prototype tested. Grants secured. Media coverage in space journals.

Exit Strategy

Acquisition by major space firms or IPO in 5-7 years.
